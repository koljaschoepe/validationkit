# Operations Playbook v0

> **Status:** v0 — 2 chapter skeletons. Phase-0-Gate Criterion #9 (PRD §6.5). Sales collateral for the Operations Sprint ($4,500).

> **Voice:** Skeptic-Mentor for the Agency-Lena persona. Less "founder hustle," more "consultant who has seen the agency mistakes before."

## Who this is for

This playbook is the operations counterpart to the Validation Handbook. Where the Handbook is for solo founders trying to find product-market-fit, the Playbook is for **AI-Consultancies and Boutique-Agencies** (Persona "Agency-Lena," 8–25 MA) who have **already found their PMF** and now need to keep 5–30 customer-repos aligned.

If you are:
- An agency owner with 4+ active customer projects, each running its own AI-agent stack,
- A consultant who's been burned by template drift across customers,
- A solo-consultant graduating from one-off projects to a productized practice,

then this Playbook maps to the same 2-week, $4,500 Operations Sprint that solo founders get from the Validation Handbook.

## Chapters

1. **[Customer Onboarding](./01-onboarding.md)** — first 14 days. The Sprint deliverables.
2. **[Template Distribution](./02-template-distribution.md)** — the PR-Workflow. Keep customer repos aligned without dropping into each one weekly.
3. **[Compliance-Frame Customers](./03-compliance-frame.md)** — Pharma / Finance / Marketing-with-PII (Track-C1 Strong-Pain-Segment). $8,500 sprint + $1,500/qtr retainer.

## What the Playbook does *not* cover

- Hiring / scaling beyond 25 people (Phase 3 territory; not Phase-0).
- Generic agency-sales / lead-gen advice (already abundant elsewhere).
- LLM model selection / prompt-engineering (your domain).

What it covers is the *operational layer between customer signature and customer delivery* — the part most agencies hand-roll and quietly fail at.
