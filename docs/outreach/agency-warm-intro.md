# Agency — Warm Intro

> **Use when:** friend-of-friend or industry-connection introduction.
>
> **Channel:** email (preferred). LinkedIn DM only if their email isn't gettable.
>
> **Audience:** AI-Consultancy / Boutique-Agency CEO or founder. 4–30 people. DACH-located.

## Template

```
Subject: Cross-vendor agent-files — quick research question

Hi [first name],

[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out. I'm researching how DACH AI
agencies keep agent-files (CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md / .cursor/rules) aligned
across multi-customer engagements.

I'm doing 10 short conversations with agency founders. 25 minutes, structured.
Specific topic: what's the substitute for keeping templates in sync today,
and what does it cost you per quarter.

No pitch attached. The output is a handbook chapter; your name appears only
if you opt-in.

Tuesday Nov 18 at 11:00 or Thursday Nov 20 at 15:00 CET?

If neither works, ignore — no offence taken.

— Kolja
[link to your personal site, NOT to a product]
```

## Differences from the Indie warm-intro

| Element | Indie | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Duration ask | 15 min | **25 min** (agencies have multi-customer complexity to unpack) |
| Subject | "validate side-projects" | "Cross-vendor agent-files" (technical-specific) |
| Topic framing | "build/don't-build calls" | "keep templates in sync today" (operational) |
| Output proof | "handbook" | "handbook chapter; opt-in attribution" |
| Reply rate target | 50–80% (warm) | 50–80% (warm) — both warm intros perform similarly |

## Pre-flight before sending

- [ ] Mutual connection actually consented to being named. (Don't drop names without permission.)
- [ ] Agency is in the 4–30 person band (verify on their About page / LinkedIn).
- [ ] At least 1 customer-engagement evidence visible publicly (case study, talk, podcast).
- [ ] Agency does AI-Consultancy work explicitly (not "we use AI internally" only).
- [ ] Two named slots actually in your calendar.

## The LOI-funnel

Warm-intro interviews aren't the LOI step yet. They're discovery. But the path is:

1. **Discovery interview (25 min).** Surface real substitute + cost.
2. **Follow-up demo (45 min).** If their substitute genuinely costs them >5h/week, demo the audit + drift flow.
3. **Operations-Sprint proposal.** $4,500, 14 days, fixed scope. (See Playbook ch 1.)
4. **LOI signed.** Phase-0-Gate Criterion #3.

Don't compress this. Each step earns the next. Compressing kills the LOI rate.

## What you don't do

- Don't send a product overview deck. Wrong moment.
- Don't quote pricing in the first message. Wrong moment.
- Don't ask "would you be interested in a tool…". Wrong tense.
- Don't include 5 testimonials. You don't have them yet.
- Don't open with "I noticed your agency is doing great work…". Read as flattery.

## What happens after they say yes

```
Great. Calendar invite for [confirmed slot] coming through. Topic structure:

1. Walk me through how you onboarded your most recent customer.
2. What did you do before that to set up agent-files?
3. What tooling did you try? What worked, what didn't?
4. How much time per quarter goes to template-drift cleanup?
5. What happens when a customer pushes back on a template change?

No prep required. We'll just talk through these.

— Kolja
```

The 5 questions map to Mom-Test stems adapted for multi-customer context.
