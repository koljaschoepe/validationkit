# Indie — Build-in-Public Public-Reply

> **Use when:** they posted in public about a struggle / decision related to validation.
>
> **Channel:** the same public thread, then a DM.
>
> **Reply-rate expectation:** 20–40% public-reply → DM accept.

## The 2-step flow

### Step 1 — Public reply (1–2 sentences max)

Reply *in the thread* with a specific, useful, NON-pitch comment. No link to ValidationKit. No "DM me." Just one helpful observation.

**Examples:**

Their post: *"4 months in, my dev-productivity tool has 3 paid users at $9. Considering pivot. What would you do?"*

**Good public reply:**
> Three at $9 is 17% reply-rate equivalent on cold-mail. Not signal yet. What did the other ~25 people who tried it say in the cancel-flow? That's the data, not the count.

Their post: *"Should I build the auth flow first or the demo?"*

**Good public reply:**
> Auth flow first costs 3 days you don't have yet. Hardcoded demo first costs 1 hour and tells you if the promise is even demanded. The auth is a Sprint-3 thing.

### Step 2 — DM 24h later

After the public reply gets engagement (or even if not), DM them within 24 hours:

```
Hey [first name],

Saw your thread on [topic]. I'm researching how solo founders make
build/don't-build calls in DACH — your post hit one of the patterns I'm
trying to triangulate.

15 min, no pitch, no follow-up unless you want one. Tuesday Nov 18 at
14:00 or Thursday Nov 20 at 10:00 CET?

— Kolja
```

Short. References the public moment. Same boundaries.

## Why this works

**Reciprocity proof:** you gave value in public before asking for value in private. The recipient saw the comment land. They might've liked it. They've already evaluated whether you're a real human with a real opinion.

**Self-selection:** people who engage with the public reply are predisposed to take the DM seriously. People who don't engage probably wouldn't have replied to a cold DM either.

## What NOT to do (mistakes that kill the channel)

| Anti-pattern | Why it backfires |
|---|---|
| Public reply that name-drops ValidationKit | Read as ambush marketing. |
| Public reply with a link | Read as ad. |
| Public reply that's generic ("interesting!") | Lost in noise. |
| Public reply with a poll / Twitter retweet | Reads as engagement-farming. |
| DM that doesn't reference the public moment | Wastes the bridge. |
| DM that says "as I mentioned in the thread…" | Pushy. Cite once, then move on. |

## Pace

- Maximum **2 public-reply attempts per day.** This is conversation, not blast.
- Maximum **5 DM-follow-ups per week.** Manual. Researched. PRD Constraint #3.
- Track in `recruitment.md` which row the public-reply maps to. If the DM gets accepted, status → `outreach-sent`. If ignored, status stays `not-yet`.

## When they reply publicly with "want to chat? DM me"

That's you, faster. DM immediately:

```
Hey — saw the reply, thanks for being open. Same as in the thread: 15 min,
no pitch, no follow-up unless you want one. Tuesday Nov 18 at 14:00 or
Thursday Nov 20 at 10:00 CET?

— Kolja
```

Skip the recap. They came to you.
