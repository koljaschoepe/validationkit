# Indie — Cold But Specific

> **Use when:** no mutual connection, but you've read at least one of their posts / shipped projects in detail.
>
> **Channel:** email if you can find one. X / LinkedIn DM otherwise.
>
> **Reply-rate expectation:** 8–15% per Track-D1 priors. Below 5% = personalization is weak.

## Template

```
Subject: Your post on [exact topic] — small question

Hi [first name],

I read your [post / thread / repo] on [exact topic] last week — the part where
you mentioned [ONE specific detail they said] stuck with me, because it
matches a pattern I'm researching across DACH solo founders.

I'm doing ~20 short interviews (15 min, no pitch, no follow-up unless you want
one) to understand how solo founders actually decide what to build.

If you'd be open: Tuesday Nov 18 at 14:00 or Thursday Nov 20 at 10:00 CET?

If neither works or this isn't useful, ignore — no offence taken.

— Kolja
[link to your personal site — NOT to a product]
```

## What makes this work (vs. typical cold-DM)

**Wrong:** "Hi {{firstName}}, I came across your profile and was impressed by your work…"

**Why it fails:** Generic + flatter-first. Marks it as automation.

**Right:** "I read your [post] on [exact topic]…the part where you mentioned [specific detail] stuck with me…"

**Why it works:** Demonstrates real reading. The recipient knows you spent ≥10 minutes on them before sending.

## The "specific detail" — what counts

| Specific detail | Why it works |
|---|---|
| A line from a tweet they wrote 2 weeks ago | Recent + verifiable. |
| A trade-off they described in a podcast | Hard to fake — you actually listened. |
| A choice they made in a public repo | Concrete + technical. |
| A retro they posted ("I shipped X, here's what I learned") | They'll appreciate the engagement. |

**What doesn't count:** their bio. their pinned tweet. Their "I'm building [vague]" line. These are LinkedIn-DM-territory.

## Pre-flight before sending

- [ ] You've read the referenced post for real and could discuss it for 60 seconds.
- [ ] The specific detail is in the post, not invented.
- [ ] You're sending fewer than 5 of these per day. (Manual outreach respects rate. Mass = spam.)
- [ ] You're not sending it through automation. (PRD Constraint #3.)
- [ ] Tracker row in `recruitment.md` notes the post you referenced — so a follow-up months later doesn't re-cite the same thing.

## When they reply with "what's this for?"

```
Fair. I'm researching how DACH solo founders make build/don't-build calls before
they commit code. Aggregating it for a handbook — your name won't appear unless
you explicitly opt-in. No pitch attached.

Still up for 15 min on [time]?
```

Direct, doesn't dodge the question, doesn't apologize.

## When they reply with "send me the questions and I'll answer in email"

That's a yes-but. Send the 5 stems (see Handbook Ch 2). Some of the best Mom-Test data is async — and an email reply tells you the substitute they describe is *real enough that they don't need a video call to recall it.*
