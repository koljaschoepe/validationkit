# Indie — Warm Intro

> **Use when:** mutual connection exists, you've been briefly introduced or have crossed paths.
>
> **Channel:** email (preferred) or LinkedIn / X DM.

## Template

```
Subject: Quick question on how you validate side-projects

Hi [first name],

[Mutual connection]'s name came up when I was looking through your work on
[ONE specific project they shipped]. Hope they're doing well.

I'm researching how solo founders in DACH validate ideas before they build.
Talking to ~20 people, no pitch, no follow-up unless you want one.

Could I grab 15 minutes — Tuesday Nov 18 at 14:00 or Thursday Nov 20 at 10:00 CET?
I'll send you the 5 questions ahead so we don't waste time.

If neither works or this isn't useful right now, just ignore — no offence taken.

— Kolja
```

## Why each line earns its place

| Line | Earns |
|---|---|
| Subject — "Quick question on how you validate side-projects" | Specific, ≤ 50 chars, not a pitch. |
| Mutual-connection mention | Reciprocity proof. The recipient checks if you're real. |
| ONE specific project | Personalization proof. Generic "I've seen your work" gets ignored. |
| "Talking to ~20 people, no pitch, no follow-up" | Sets boundaries up front. Lowers cost of accepting. |
| **Two named time-slots** | Removes scheduling friction. Don't say "any time that works." |
| "5 questions ahead" | Signals it's structured, not a meandering chat. |
| "Just ignore" closer | Permission to say no = higher reply rate (counter-intuitive but holds). |

## Pre-flight before sending

- [ ] Mutual connection actually exists (you didn't imagine it).
- [ ] The specific project is real and shipped (not their LinkedIn bio).
- [ ] The two time-slots are *actually* in your calendar.
- [ ] You have the 5 questions written and ready to send post-accept.
- [ ] Tracker row in `recruitment.md` updated to `outreach-sent` with the date.

## What you DON'T do

- Don't include a link to ValidationKit / Sondr. This is research, not promo.
- Don't mention the $4,500 sprint. Wrong moment.
- Don't ask "would you be interested in a tool that…". Future-tense kills.
- Don't follow up if they don't reply within 7 days. One ask, one chance.

## What happens after they say yes

Send within 1 hour:

```
Great. I'll send a calendar invite for [confirmed slot]. The 5 questions, so you
can think about them:

1. Walk me through the last time you tried to validate an idea.
2. What did you do before that?
3. What did you try? What worked, what didn't?
4. How long did it take? Was that a lot, or normal?
5. What happened after?

No prep required. We'll just talk through these. Looking forward.

— Kolja
```

Done.
