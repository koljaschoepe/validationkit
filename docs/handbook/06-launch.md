# 6 — Launch

**Thesis:** The launch is not a moment. It's a 30-day window of *engineered surface area* — and the first 3 days carry 60% of the lift.

## Concession-then-Critique

**Concession:** Product Hunt launches and "I'm launching today!" threads still work for some products. Vanity numbers (upvotes, retweets) feel like signal. They feel like progress.

**Critique:** Most of those signals don't convert. PH-launches average ~5% click-through, ~0.5% sign-up, ~0.05% paid conversion. That's 100 visits per paid customer in the best case. If you have 10k upvotes, you'd convert ~5 paid customers. Vanity ≠ revenue. Engineer the *narrow* surface — warm intros, build-in-public momentum, ICP-specific posts — not the broad one.

## The 30-day shape

```
  Day 0 ─────── Day 30
  ════════
  │ │  │
  │ │  └── Compound: SEO indexes, content matures, intros multiply
  │ └───── Conversion: 5-7 customers landed via your engineered channels
  └─────── Bang: 3 days of engineered surface area
```

**Days 1–3: Bang.** All channels firing. Warm intros sent in advance, content scheduled, demo screenshots ready, pricing-page final. The 3-day window earns ~60% of the 30-day attention.

**Days 4–14: Conversion.** Engineered surface lands warm leads in a Calendly. You do ~10 demo calls. 5–7 sign up. Their feedback shapes Days 15–30.

**Days 15–30: Compound.** Content goes live (1 long-form / week). SEO starts to index. Warm intros from happy customers compound. You start the next sprint.

## The day-0 checklist (30 items, do them all)

**Demo + Product**
- [ ] Demo URL works on a clean browser, no auth required.
- [ ] One-screenshot story tells the wedge in 5 seconds.
- [ ] Pricing page is final. No "TBD."
- [ ] First-customer-flow is sub-90-seconds end-to-end.
- [ ] Brand/voice consistent across docs, pricing, demo.

**Warm Intros (the 60%)**
- [ ] 20 founders / customers already DM'd a week ago with "soft launch."
- [ ] 5 of them committed to a public mention or RT.
- [ ] 2 podcast appearances or interviews scheduled in the launch window.
- [ ] 1 newsletter (yours or partner) goes out Day 1.
- [ ] Anti-cold-DM rule respected (PRD §2 Constraint #3).

**Build-in-Public Content**
- [ ] 60 days of pre-launch posts (~5/wk × 12wk) consistent in voice.
- [ ] Launch-day thread written and scheduled.
- [ ] 1 deep-dive post (the "build" thread, with screenshots).
- [ ] 1 honest "what's not yet" post (Trust-Page-like).
- [ ] Cross-posted to X + LinkedIn + Mastodon.

**Press / Long-tail**
- [ ] HN "Show HN" post scheduled for a US-morning hour.
- [ ] Reddit (1 specific niche subreddit) post — not 5.
- [ ] Product Hunt: *optional*, not the primary lever.

**Analytics + Funnel**
- [ ] You can see visits → demo → Calendly → paid in one dashboard.
- [ ] You can answer "where did the last conversion come from?" by tomorrow morning.
- [ ] You've decided which metric goes in the Day-30 retro.

**Personal Capacity**
- [ ] Calendar cleared Days 1–3.
- [ ] Inbox triage rule: only customer + press messages get a real reply.
- [ ] Backup founder/friend on standby for emotional support / sanity check.
- [ ] Sleep 7+ hours the night before launch.

## Skeptic-Mentor counter-example

A founder spent 4 weeks "preparing the launch": polishing copy, redesigning logo, drafting Show-HN post. Launched on a Tuesday. Got 220 visits in 24h, 7 sign-ups, 0 paid.

The retrospective was honest: zero warm intros, zero podcast appearances, zero pre-launch content. He'd polished the product surface; he hadn't built the *distribution* surface. The 4 weeks should've been split: 1 week polish, 3 weeks of warm-intro DMs and content. The launch isn't the build — it's the *broadcasting of the build to people who already trust you*.

## Done-when

- 5+ paid customers landed in the 30-day window. (Below 5 means the channel didn't work — see chapter 3.)
- One specific narrative is told in 1 sentence about the launch: "we landed N because we did X." If you can't say it, you can't repeat it.
- One specific *not*-narrative is told: "we did Y and it produced nothing. We won't do Y again."
- The Day-30 retro is written. Not by Day 32. Day 30.

If the retro isn't written, you've already lost the second sprint of learning.
